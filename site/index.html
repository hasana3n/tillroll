Test printing the way your app really prints.

Tillroll stands in for an ESC/POS thermal printer and speaks the same protocol, so you test the bytes your app sends, the layout they produce and how your app handles a printer in trouble.

Connect

It listens on TCP port 9100 as soon as it starts. Serial, USB and Bluetooth are a click away.

Print

Each receipt appears as it arrives, one screen pixel per printer dot, with its time and length on the arrivals board.

Break it on purpose

Open the cover, run out of paper or raise an error. Status queries get real answers and jobs are dropped, like on real hardware.

Your computer becomes a Bluetooth printer.

On Windows and Linux, start a Bluetooth row and print straight from your phone app, the same way it would print to a handheld printer.

Android

Classic Bluetooth with the standard SPP UUID. Pair once in the phone's settings.

iPhone and iPad

Bluetooth LE with the common printer service 18F0. No pairing needed.

A test client is built in.

The Monitor prints sample receipts, every barcode, QR, PDF417, DataMatrix and Aztec, opens the cash drawer and sounds the buzzer. It shows the status Tillroll reports back, so you see what your app would see.
